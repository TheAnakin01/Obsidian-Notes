# Households share the list, not the plan

- **Context:** The idea was "family shares one plan and a live shopping list".
- **Problem:** Family members have different allergies, diets and calorie needs — one shared plan could be unsafe.
- **Decision:** Everyone keeps their **own** plan; the household shares **one combined shopping list** that updates
  live (Supabase Realtime). Members see only each other's chosen names — never email, plan, allergies or weight.
- **Consequences:** Safe by design; up to 8 people; invite codes last 7 days.
