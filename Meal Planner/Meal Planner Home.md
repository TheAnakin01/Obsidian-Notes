# 🥗 Meal Planner — Home

A free, mobile-friendly meal planning app for India: daily calorie & macro targets, a **week of allergy-safe meals**,
a shopping list with grocery-app links, food diary, progress charts, an AI coach, reminders, family sharing — and it
works offline as an installed app. Built 2026-09-27 → 2026-09-28 with Claude Code. **Cost: ₹0/month.**
